# Category photography

These photographs illustrate **categories**, not individual products, stock, product packaging, or store inventory. They must not be reused as missing product photographs. Admin-uploaded category images override these bundled assets.

The source pages below were checked for reusable licensing on 11 November 2026. Each photograph retains its own license. Adapted CC BY-SA photographs are distributed under the corresponding same/share-alike license, with attribution and change information.

Local versions are resized, without enlargement, and re-encoded as WebP for the category UI. Transparent areas are composited over white. No AI-generated objects, labels, or product claims have been added. All photographs remain attributable using the source manifest [`public/categories/sources.json`](./public/categories/sources.json).
